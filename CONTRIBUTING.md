# Contributing

Thanks for checking out the project!

I am currently not accepting Pull Requests. I want to keep this project as a solo effort for now to maintain a specific direction.

**However, I’d love your feedback!**
* **Found a bug?** Please open an Issue.
* **Have an idea?** Open a "Feature Request" issue.
* **Want to change the code?** Feel free to fork the repo and build your own version!

Thanks for understanding.
